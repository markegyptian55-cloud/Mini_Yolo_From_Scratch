Apples-to-apples baseline: EXPERIMENT 1 model evaluated on EXPERIMENT 2's clean test split
============================================================================================
Why this file exists
--------------------
Experiment 2 trains on `dataset_clean/`, a convention-consistent subset of the
original dataset (see src/v2/tools/make_clean_dataset.py). Its val/test splits are
SUBSETS of the originals, so exp2's numbers cannot be compared directly against
exp1's numbers on the original test split. To make the comparison valid, the
exp1 checkpoint was re-evaluated on exp2's clean test split. That is the row
exp2 must beat.

Command used
------------
python -m src.v2.val --weights runs/v2/v2_n384/weights/best.pt \
    --data dataset_clean/data.yaml --split test

Reading
-------
Exp1 scores WORSE here (mAP50-95 0.473) than on its own mixed test split
(0.541). open_eye collapses hardest: 0.355 here vs 0.543 on the mixed split,
and mAP50 0.763 vs 0.881. That is the expected signature of the annotation-
convention problem -- on the mixed split exp1 could score easy points on the
big face-scale boxes; with those removed, only genuine per-eye localization
counts, and it is much weaker at that. yawning is unchanged (0.651 vs 0.650),
exactly as predicted, since no yawning boxes were removed by the filter.
